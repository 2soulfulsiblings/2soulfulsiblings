    4. Listing quality checklist

    • Title leads with the main search phrase
    • All 13 tags used, mix of short and long phrases
    • First photo shows the product clearly, with text readable at thumbnail size
    • Description says exactly what files they receive, format and size
    • Digital download notes: no physical item, how to download
    • Price checked against 5 similar listings
    • You read the AI copy out loud and fixed anything stiff
    • Only your own artwork, or assets with a license that allows resale

    Always read Etsy's current digital product and AI disclosure rules before publishing. They change.
