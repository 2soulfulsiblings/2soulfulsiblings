# How to Build Your Own Challenge Funnel (the Teaching Guide)

This is the method behind the Christmas challenge pages. Anyone can copy the *structure* and fill it with their own niche, products and voice.

## The 5 piece funnel
1. **Landing page** (`index.html`): one promise, one email form.
2. **Free gift** (`free/index.html`): delivered right after signup.
3. **Challenge**: a daily action list that builds a habit and trust.
4. **Product ladder** (`products/index.html`): free, low price, core offer, premium, membership.
5. **Email follow up**: a short welcome series that points to the ladder.

## Landing page recipe (top to bottom)
Hero with promise and form, "sound familiar" pain points, how it works in 4 steps, the daily roadmap, what is free, about the host, FAQ, repeat the form.

## Picking your promise
Formula: **[Result] in [timeframe], with [tool or method], without [main pain].**
Example: 25 Christmas listings in 25 days, with AI, without staring at a blank page.

## Make the free gift useful in 10 minutes
A tracker, a list of ideas, a few prompts and a checklist. If someone can use it tonight, they trust you tomorrow.

## The product ladder
| Tier | Job | Suggested price |
|---|---|---|
| Free kit | Earn trust and the email | $0 |
| Prompt pack | Easy first purchase | $7 to $12 |
| Workbook + templates | Core offer | $19 to $37 |
| Done with you vault | Premium | $47 to $97 |
| Membership | Recurring | $12 to $29/month |

Test your own prices. These are starting points, not research.

## Going live checklist
- [ ] Set `FORM_ACTION` in `app.js` to your email provider's form endpoint (until then signups stay in the visitor's browser and no emails are collected).
- [ ] Replace each `href="#"` on the products page with a real checkout link.
- [ ] Add your real story and photo, and your real refund and license terms.
- [ ] Only add testimonials and numbers that are real.
- [ ] Add a privacy policy and an unsubscribe process (email laws apply).
- [ ] Check Etsy's current rules on digital products and AI disclosure.
