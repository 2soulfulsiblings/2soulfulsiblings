Questions


Do I need design skills?

No. Everything is built around Canva and simple templates.

Is it too late to start for this Christmas?

Start where you are. Buyers keep shopping for digital downloads right up to the last days. Many of these also work as New Year products.

Can I really do this in 30 to 45 minutes a day?

Most days, yes. Plan on a few slower days and catch up on the weekend.

Will AI make my listings sound generic?

Only if you skip the edit. Every prompt has a "make it sound like me" step.
